# Roadmap

## Phase 0: Feasibility
Prototype detection for Reels, Shorts, Spotlight and YouTube channel on a real phone. Exit: each surface detected reliably across two app versions; channel read works for search, autoplay and shared-link entry.

## Phase 1: Core blocking
Foreground service, rules engine, closed-feed screen, website blocklist, setup guide for permissions. Exit: P0 blocking works for a full day without crashes.

## Phase 2: Sessions
Focus sessions, locked mode, emergency unlock with wait, schedules. Exit: a locked session cannot be bypassed by normal means.

## Phase 3: Insights and welcome
Welcome walkthrough, Today screen, year grid, projections labelled. Exit: numbers match logged events.

## Phase 4: Hardening and release
Detector health, update resilience, accessibility of the UI, free distribution route. Exit: closed beta feedback addressed.

## Later
Weekly and monthly stats, local export, more apps and surfaces.

## Gates that can change the plan
Reliable selective blocking, a zero-cost distribution route, and detector upkeep when apps update.
