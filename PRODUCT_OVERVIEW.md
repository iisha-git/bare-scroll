# Product Overview

## Problem
Short-form feeds are built to keep people scrolling. Blocking a whole app is too blunt: people still need YouTube for lectures or Instagram for messages. Existing blockers that do this well are paid or limited.

## Solution
Bare Scroll blocks the feed, not the app. The user keeps messages, search, and the creators they chose, and loses the endless loop.

## Audience
Students and anyone who wants to scroll less without deleting apps. Android phones first.

## Principles
1. Selective: block the feed surface, not the whole app.
2. Free: no paywall for core features.
3. Private: no account, no upload of usage data.
4. Honest: projections are labelled as projections; sample numbers are never shown as real.
5. Calm: a quiet black, white and grey interface with plain sans-serif type.

## In scope
Reels, Shorts and Spotlight blocking. YouTube channel blocklist. Custom website blocklist. Focus sessions. Delayed emergency unlock. Welcome walkthrough and usage insights.

## Out of scope (for now)
iOS, accounts and cloud sync, social or leaderboard features, parental controls.

## Success measures
Fewer short-form opens per day for a returning user. Blocking works on current versions of the target apps. Users do not need to disable the app to get things done.

## Open questions
Reliable channel identification, free distribution route, resilience to target-app updates. See [BLOCKING_APPROACH.md](BLOCKING_APPROACH.md) and [ROADMAP.md](ROADMAP.md).
